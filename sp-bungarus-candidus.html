What a bite does

Progressive neuromuscular paralysis leading to respiratory failure; unusually high fatality rate persists even after antivenom treatment, worse than most other entries in this table.

If you are bitten

Get to a hospital that holds antivenom. Keep still, remove rings and watches, and do not cut, suck or tie off the bite. Use the red “Bitten?” button at the top of any page.
